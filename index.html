Frequently asked questions

Are your courses KHDA approved?

All courses are delivered under Mentorix Academy's KHDA-licensed Training Institute permit. Course content is subject to KHDA activity-permit approval.

Where can I see fees and durations?

Our full course catalog page lists every course with its duration and indicative fee in AED. Click "Course Catalog" in the menu to browse.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Most courses are available both in-centre and online, so you can learn anytime, anywhere at your own pace.

Can you train our company's team?

Absolutely. Corporate training can be arranged on-site, online, or blended, and customised to your team's goals.
